I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at The University of Hong Kong (HKU). I received my Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, and my B.Eng. from Beijing Jiaotong University, China. I am the Principal Investigator (PI) of the Network Intelligence and Computing Ecosystem (NICE) Lab @ HKU.

Our research at NICE Lab spans the following key areas of artificial intelligence and next-generation networks:

These projects cover two complementary directions:

  • Network for AI - Unfold Intelligence by Horizontally Scaling It Across the Network
  • AI for Network - Specialize Intelligence by Vertically Optimizing It for Network Systems

They converge toward a unified goal: Ubiquitous Intelligence, where intelligence is woven into every interaction, every environment, and every moment. Looking ahead, we envision a "Planet-scale Computer" with the network as its backplane.

Please refer to NICE for openings, research opportunities, and application details.
